# UK LTD Setup Guide — Logic Loop Ltd (direct GOV.UK route)

*Owner: Adam. Fees, eligibility, and ID rules change — confirm every figure on GOV.UK before paying. Tax questions go to the accountant.*

## Decisions
- Registered in the UK only. **No Jordanian commercial registration** (Fahed's decision).
- Filed directly on GOV.UK by Fahed.

## Known trade-offs to raise with the accountant
- Some Amman clients may ask for a local tax invoice or deduct withholding tax when paying a foreign company.
- A company managed from Amman may raise tax-residence questions under the UK–Jordan treaty.
- Personal tax in Jordan on money drawn from the company.

| Payment card | Online filing fee — check the current GOV.UK figure (roughly £100) |

## Step 2 — Identity verification
1. Create a GOV.UK One Login.
2. Verify with Companies House (GOV.UK ID Check app + passport).
3. Save the **Companies House personal code**.
If the app does not accept the passport, use an Authorised Corporate Service Provider (ACSP) for verification only.
