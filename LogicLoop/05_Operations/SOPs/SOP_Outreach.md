# SOP — Outreach (owner: Sara; Lina reviews)
**Purpose:** consistent, personal outreach that turns into conversations.
**Steps:**
1. Add the lead to the lead list and fill in the audit (visual quality, posting, reels, Google profile, ads).
2. Pick the angle from the audit. Every message references one specific detail about their business.
3. Draft from `06_Outreach/Scripts/Outreach_Scripts.md`, then Fahed approves the batch.
4. Fahed sends on Monday or Wednesday, 13:00–17:00, from his own accounts.
5. Follow up once after 4 days. No reply means mark it "No reply".
6. A reply counts as a conversation on the weekly scorecard. Book a meeting, then send a proposal.
7. If the client accepts or sends a contract, hand off to Fahed completely.
