# SOP — Client Onboarding (owner: Adam; runs with Omar)
**Purpose:** every new client starts the same way, within 7 days of signing.
**Trigger:** Fahed confirms the contract is signed and the first invoice is paid.
**Steps:**
1. Omar copies `04_Projects/Clients/_Client_Template/` to `04_Projects/Clients/<ClientName>/` (day 0).
2. Fahed holds the kickoff meeting; Sara fills `Client_Brief_Intake.md` from his notes (day 1–2).
3. Client grants partner access (Meta Business Manager, Google Business Profile). **Passwords are never stored** (day 1–3).
4. Rami records the baseline numbers (day 3).
5. Kareem drafts the strategy brief and shoot brief; Lina challenges them; Fahed approves (day 3–4).
6. Fahed shoots (first Sun/Tue slot).
7. The first content batch goes to Fahed, then the client, for approval (day 7).
**Done when:** every box in `Onboarding_Checklist.md` for week 1 is ticked.
