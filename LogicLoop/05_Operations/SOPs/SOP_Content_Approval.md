# SOP — Content Approval (owner: Omar)
**Purpose:** nothing is published without two approvals.
**Steps:**
1. Kareem drafts, following the content chain. Sara designs if needed.
2. Lina's challenge pass (optional for routine posts, required for campaigns).
3. Fahed approves: logged in `Approvals.md` with the date.
4. The client approves, via WhatsApp or email, and it's logged the same way.
5. Scheduled or posted only after both approvals are ticked.
**Rule:** a missing approval means it doesn't go out. No exceptions.
