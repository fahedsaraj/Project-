- Taxes: [to be confirmed with accountant — withholding tax / sales tax treatment].

## 4. Approvals
The Client approves content, ad copy, and ad budgets before publication. Content not approved or commented on within [3] working days [is treated as approved / is held] — choose one.

## 5. Client responsibilities
Provide access to social accounts and ad accounts, product/menu information, brand assets, and access to the venue for scheduled shoots.

## 6. Content ownership and use
- On full payment, the Client may use delivered content for its own marketing.
- Logic Loop retains copyright in photos and video and may show the work in its portfolio unless the Client objects in writing.
- Raw/unedited files are not included unless agreed.

## 7. Results
Logic Loop commits to the agreed deliverables and to monthly reporting. Specific sales or follower numbers are targets, not promises.

## 8. Confidentiality
Both parties keep the other's non-public business information confidential.

## 9. Termination
Either party may end the agreement for material breach not fixed within 14 days of written notice. Fees for work already delivered remain payable.
