# How Amman's leading agencies look (Sara, 2026-09-25)

**Method and limits:** Instagram only shows profiles to logged-in users. We did not log in. A cloud browser read each agency's website and confirmed the Instagram handle. Instagram grids were **not seen**. Lines marked *(unverified)* are the browser agent's reading, not confirmed.
Clutch's "top Jordan" list is led by foreign agencies that list Jordan (Sociallyin, Iron Roots, Social House, Hotspex), so we skipped them.

## What this means for us
- The market is **black/white or corporate blue**, and **wordmark-led**. A warm, confident colour will stand out in a prospect's feed.
- Nobody we saw leads with the **Arabic letterform** in the mark. That's open ground (Option 03).
- Our edge is photography (food, cafés). Warm colours sit well next to food photos. Cold blue doesn't.
