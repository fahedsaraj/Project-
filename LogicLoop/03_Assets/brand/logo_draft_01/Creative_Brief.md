# Logo draft 01 — creative brief (Sara, 2026-09-25)
*Draft, not final. Needs Fahed's approval.*

**CONCEPT:** The frame is the loop. Two camera-viewfinder corners, each an "L" (Logic, Loop), are cut on the diagonal so the frame looks like it is turning. That is the loop: shoot → publish → measure → again.
**VISUAL STORY:** A viewfinder locks onto a subject (the terracotta dot) and keeps rotating around it. The client's business stays in focus.
**HERO ELEMENT:** The terracotta dot at the centre. The eye lands there first. The same dot sits on the "i" in the wordmark.
**COMPOSITION:** Centred, 180° rotational symmetry, open corners at top-left and bottom-right to create movement. Stacked lockup: symbol → Logic Loop → لوجيك لوب.
**LIGHTING:** Flat mark (a logo must print and scale). On the board, soft top light and a low shadow give it depth for presentation only.
**COLOUR:** Charcoal #1F1D1B · warm off-white #F5F0E8 · terracotta #C65A3A (accent only, one touch per design) · sand #E6DCCB (support).
**TYPOGRAPHY:** English: bold geometric sans, tight tracking (Manrope in the render; Canva uses draft A's font). Arabic: IBM Plex Sans Arabic SemiBold in the render; Canva default Arabic bold for now. Font lock after approval.
**SUPPORTING ELEMENTS:** None in the mark. Your photography carries colour and texture everywhere else.
**COPY:** Logic Loop / لوجيك لوب
**FORMAT:** Logo 2000×2000 (Canva); board 1080×1350; profile picture 1080×1080.
**FINAL ART DIRECTION:** A quiet, confident mark. Two L-shaped frame corners rotate around one orange point, the thing being shot and measured. Readable at 56 px on a phone.

## Checks

- Readable at profile size? Yes, tested at 150 / 110 / 56 px.
