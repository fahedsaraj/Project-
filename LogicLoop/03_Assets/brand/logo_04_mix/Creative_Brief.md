# Logo 04 — the mix (Sara, 2026-09-25)
*Draft, not final. Fahed asked: "Mix the three options and the two options. And make one."*

**CONCEPT:** One line writes both names. It draws the Arabic **ل** (stem + bowl) and the English **L** (stem + foot), and on the way it ties a **loop**: shoot → publish → measure → again.
**FROM EACH OPTION:** 03 → the shared ل/L stem (the idea) · 02 → the single looping line and the sunset colours · 01 → the dot, now in terracotta-coral on the "i" of Logic.
**HERO ELEMENT:** The loop where the line crosses the stem.
**COMPOSITION:** Mark above, bilingual wordmark below (stacked). Horizontal version for banners.
**LIGHTING:** Flat mark. On the board, a soft coral glow behind it on night background for depth.
**COLOUR:** Night #1A0F1F · gradient saffron #F2B138 → coral #FF5B3A → magenta #E0307A · cream #FFF4E8. Flat version: coral #FF5B3A.
**TYPOGRAPHY:** Readex Pro SemiBold for both English and Arabic (one family = one brand). Coral dot on the "i".
**COPY:** Logic Loop / لوجيك لوب
**FORMAT:** Board 1080×1350 · profile 1080×1080 · Canva 2000×2000.

## Checks
- Readable at 56 px: yes.
- Risk: the ل puts the stem right of the bowl, so English readers may also see a "d". That is the Arabic letter's own shape. Kept, not distorted.
- Canva version is flat coral (gradient shapes aren't possible through the connector) and uses draft A's font until fonts are locked.
