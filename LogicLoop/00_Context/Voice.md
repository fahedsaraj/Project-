# Voice — Logic Loop

## Tone
**Grounded, confident, clear, and direct.** Focus on measurable ROI, clean visual quality, and real strategy.

## Two registers

| Register | Used for | Language |
|---|---|---|
| **External — official** | Proposals, contracts, pitch decks, agency communications | Professional English and clear Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) |
| **External — social** | Captions, visual scripts, reels, DMs/WhatsApp outreach | Modern English and natural, localized Jordanian Arabic. Never a direct/literal translation. |
| **Internal** | Briefs, logs, reviews, notes to Fahed | English, short, structured, no filler |

## Hard rules
- **Banned words:** "premium", "viral", "guaranteed", "unbeatable". No hype or fluff words in general.
- **Emojis:** do not overuse. None in proposals or contracts.
- **Register:** never overly casual with high-end clients.
- **Currency:** always `JOD [Amount]` — e.g. `JOD 450`. Never "450 JD" or "450 dinars".
- **Sign-off** on official communications, templates, and proposals:

  > Regards,
  > Logic Loop Team

- **Format:** clear section headers, bold key concepts, bullet points, tables for pricing and schedules.

## Right and wrong

| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right |
|---|---|
| "Premium content that goes viral, guaranteed!" 🔥🔥 | "Content shot for your menu, measured by bookings and messages." |
| "Packages start from 450 JD" | "Packages start from JOD 450" |
| MSA in an Instagram caption: "نحن نقدم لكم أفضل الأطباق" | Jordanian dialect: "جرّبتوا طبقنا الجديد؟ مستنيينكم اليوم" |
| Casual in a proposal: "Hey! Super excited to work with u" | "Thank you for your time. Please find our proposal below." |
| Literal translation of an English slogan | Rewritten natively for the Amman audience |
