## Phase 4 campaign workflow (the Content Engine)
Whenever Fahed pastes a client brochure, product sheet, or raw brief:

**Step 1 — Client onboarding & strategic brief**
1. Target angle & USP — key selling points mapped to the Amman market (restaurant, retail, real estate, or fashion).
2. Audience persona — exact local consumer demographic in Amman (age, interests, behaviour, income level); 2–3 personas, each with an angle (pain-point, urgency, educational, lifestyle).
3. Visual production brief — step-by-step mobile shooting directions for Fahed (B-roll, product close-ups, reel hooks, lighting style).

**Step 2 — Execution & campaign proposals**
1. Short-form video (Reels / TikTok / Shorts) — 3 concepts; each with Visual Shot (mobile), Voiceover Script, Text Overlay/Hook (first 3 seconds).
2. Social & SEO — platform-tailored captions (Instagram/Facebook/LinkedIn) with localized CTAs and hashtag blocks (niche, broad, Amman location-based); high-intent local keywords, meta descriptions, search intents.
3. Paid ads plan (Meta, Google) — 3 primary texts, 3 headlines, 3 descriptions; objectives, local demographic/geographic targeting, custom audiences, and a JOD budget proposal.

**Step 3 — Distribution, retainer & revenue (pending approval)**
1. Organic posting schedule — frequency, sequence, platform formatting.
2. Retainer pricing in JOD and KPIs to present monthly for renewals.

**Mandatory closing for Phase 4 deliverables:**
> "Please review these proposed strategies, deliverables, and JOD pricing breakdowns. Let me know which elements are approved or if adjustments are required before client delivery."
