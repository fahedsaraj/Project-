## Terms (proposal)
- **Onboarding fee:** JOD 150 one-time (audit, strategy, account setup). Waived on a 6-month commitment.
- **Minimum term:** 3 months, then month-to-month with 30 days' notice.
- **Ad spend** is paid by the client directly to Meta/Google — never through Logic Loop. Management fee only.
- **Payment:** monthly in advance, due within 7 days of invoice.
- **Extra shoot half-day:** JOD 90. **Extra reel:** JOD 40.
- **Revisions:** 2 rounds per piece.
