# Meta ad: "Don't take our word for it" (profile visits → registrations), 2026-09-29
Made by Noor at Fahed's request (ad setup is Rami's area; a proposal only). Design: `AD_SeeOurPage_1080x1350.png` (4:5 feed) · source `ad.html` · grid thumbnails in `tiles/`.

## Idea
Every education ad says "we're the best". This one says **لا تاخذ كلامنا… شوف صفحتنا بنفسك** ("Don't take our word for it… see our page for yourself").
The visual is a phone showing their Instagram grid (our Success4Sure designs). A confident challenge plus a preview creates curiosity, the tap goes to their profile, and the profile does the selling.

## Ad copy (Meta)
**Primary text**
> لا تاخذ كلامنا… شوف صفحتنا بنفسك 👀
> كورسات AP و EST II و Digital SAT في خلدا.
> زور صفحتنا وشوف شو منقدّم، واحكي معنا على واتساب لما تكون جاهز.
>
> Don't take our word for it. See our page.
> AP, EST II and Digital SAT prep in Khalda.

**Headline:** See what we do: @success4sure_khalda
**CTA button:** Instagram placements → "Visit Instagram profile" · Facebook placements → "Send WhatsApp message"

## Flags: before running
1. **Run it only after the grid shows the new posts.** The ad promises "see our page"; if the real page looks different, the promise breaks. The phone grid uses our designs.
2. The avatar "S4S KHALDA" and the logo are stand-ins; swap in the official logo.
