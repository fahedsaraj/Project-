## 1. Creative concept: "The Answer Sheet"
EST II is a bubble-sheet exam. The background is a faint answer sheet, with a few bubbles filled in and the grid fading into the dark edges. It says "exam" without adding any text. The exam name is the hero: **EST II** in a large, dimensional serif that echoes their own serif wordmark. Everything below it walks the eye down one path: **EST II → Revision Week → Starting This Saturday → Register Now**.

## 3. Visual direction
- One focal point: "EST II", about 40% of the canvas width at its visual center
- Depth comes from a stacked-edge extrusion on the letters, soft cast shadows, a warm spotlight behind the headline, a vignette, and fine film grain (a printed finish, not flat digital)
- The date sits on the only light surface in the frame, so it is the second thing the eye lands on
- Minimal text: 8 short lines in total

## 4. Typography
| Role | Font | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Display (EST II, This Saturday) | Playfair Display Black / Semibold Italic | A high-contrast serif that echoes their serif "SUCCESS" wordmark and reads as academic |
| Labels, CTA | Karla ExtraBold, wide tracking, all caps | Their own web font, continuing their all-caps program-name habit |
| Support line | Playfair Display Italic | A softer, human counterpoint |
