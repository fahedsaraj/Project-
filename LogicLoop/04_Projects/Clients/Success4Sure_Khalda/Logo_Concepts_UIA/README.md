## Name flags (for Fahed — legal checks are Fahed's)
- **Confusion risk:** *The International Academy – Amman* (IAA, iaa.edu.jo) is a well-known IB school. "United International Academy" (UIA) is close in name and acronym. Hence the logo leads with "United" and a strong symbol.
- Other "United International School"s exist abroad (Malaysia, Bangalore, Shanghai). Low local risk.
- Unverified: whether "Academy"/"International" in a centre's name needs Ministry approval in Jordan, and whether the name is free to register. Check with a lawyer / the registration authority before committing.
