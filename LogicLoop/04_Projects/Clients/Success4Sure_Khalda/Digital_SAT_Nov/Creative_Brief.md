## Concept
A teacher-led launch post. People sign up for people, so the two teachers are the focal point, in matched portrait cards. It uses the same system as the approved EST II V2 post (dark maroon, one red key light, Inter Tight + Instrument Serif) so the two sit together on the grid as one campaign family.
