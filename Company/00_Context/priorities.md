# Priorities

> Revisit at every weekly review (Thursday) and at the 30/60/90-day checkpoints: **5 Nov 2026, 5 Dec 2026, 5 Jan 2027**.

## 90-day outcome (to ~5 Jan 2027)
Launch United International Academy as a strong, professional, recognisable international education brand, and build a working marketing and lead-generation system around it.
**Business objective:** more qualified leads, converted into enrollments. Followers and views are useful, never the goal.

## Primary: do now
1. **P01 Foundation & Transition**, in Fahed's order:
   - Team
   - Ownership
   - Access
   - Email
   - Phone
   - Logo assets
   - Social accounts
   - Brand setup
   - Content system
   - Marketing
   - Advertising
   - Leads
2. **Keep existing families**: they hear about the change first and personally, and nobody feels abandoned.
3. **Don't drop current business during the transition**: keep answering WhatsApp and calls fast, and support courses already running (SAT from 11 Oct, EST II December exam prep, AP year course).

## Secondary: do when primary isn't blocked
- A lead-tracking spine (one master contact list → CRM, with source tags on every enquiry).
- A content system: monthly calendar, templates in the new identity, reels workflow.
- A one-page website (landing + programmes + WhatsApp CTA) that links to all channels.
- Proof collection: consented results, testimonials, teacher profiles.

## The filter for new ideas
A new idea gets in now only if it answers **yes** to at least one of:
1. Does it protect trust with existing families during the transition?
2. Does it complete a foundation step in the build order?
3. Will it measurably move qualified leads → enrollments within 90 days?

If not, it goes to the Parked table with a revisit date. Atlas enforces this.
