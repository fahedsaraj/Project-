# Video edits of real footage (Fahed, 10 Oct 2026)
- Colour: a stylised auto-grade (teal/orange + vignette + saturation) was rejected. When asked to grade, do a **natural, measured per-shot correction**: white balance from a known-colour reference (shirt), skin hue ≈22–26° / sat ≈0.5, match shots to each other, luma-only contrast, no vignette/LUT look. Example: `01_Tools/brand_assets/est_econ_grade.sh`. If Fahed sends an already-graded file, keep it as delivered.
- Music and sound effects must sit **well under the voice** (sound.py `music_gain` ≈ 0.2, `fx_gain` ≈ 0.3 for talking-head ads).
- "Crazy modern" still means **professional**: clean cuts on speech pauses, restrained flashes, no gimmicks that distract from the speaker.
