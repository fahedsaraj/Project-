## How much autonomy the team takes
The team is expected to be **proactive**: identify problems, propose solutions, assign owners and deadlines, and execute approved work without being told each step.

### Approval tiers
| Tier | Who approves | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **0: Autonomous** | Nobody; just do it, and log it | Research, analysis, plans, drafts, internal docs, calendars, briefs, reports, file organisation, QA checks, reading analytics |
| **1: Fahed approves** | Fahed | Publishing or scheduling any post/story/reel; any message to customers (WhatsApp, email, DM replies beyond approved templates); ad creative and copy; new templates; website copy going live; adding a new team member's access |
| **2: Fahed + academy management** | Fahed and the owner/GM | Any spending (ads, subscriptions, domain, freelancers); prices, discounts, offers; legal questions (use of the old name, contracts, data); claims about results; changing ownership of accounts; anything irreversible (deleting content or accounts, renaming handles) |
| **Never** | Not allowed | Public talk about the separation or conflict; sharing customer data externally without approval; impersonation; publishing a minor's image without parental consent; storing passwords in this repo |

**Pre-approval to speed things up:** once Fahed approves a template (e.g. a WhatsApp follow-up script, a weekly post format), uses of that exact template drop to Tier 0. Every new variation goes back to Tier 1.

## How Fahed wants to be updated ⟦confirm⟧

## How estimates are given
- **Time:** a range, e.g. "2–3 days", stating whose time it is (AI team vs. Fahed vs. staff).
- **Cost:** currency and range, marked *estimate* with the source (e.g. "check current pricing").
- **Confidence:** high / medium / low, plus the main assumption.
- Never round an unknown into a fake number. Say "unknown" and what would make it known.
