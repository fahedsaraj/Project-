# Voice: How UIA Sounds

Source: Brand Guidelines v1.0, §13 Tone of voice, plus lessons from the 7 Oct 2026 audit. Template to refine with Fahed.

**Personality:** confident, precise, warm and international. Ambitious without being elitist.
**Two readers at once:** international students aged 15–18, and the parents trusting us with their future. Be direct enough for students and credible enough for families.
**Every piece reflects at least one value:** Excellence · Global outlook · Community · Integrity.

## Register 1: External (students, parents, public)
- **Languages:** Arabic and English, usually both in one post (Arabic first for parent-led content, English first for exam/course content). Arabic uses clear, friendly Jordanian-accessible Arabic, light dialect for reels and WhatsApp, and cleaner MSA for formal announcements and email.
- **The four rules:**

| Rule | Say | Don't say |
|---|---|---|
| **Clear:** short sentences, one idea at a time | "SAT classes start on 11 October. Places are limited." | "Prospective learners are encouraged to register in a timely manner." |
| **Ambitious:** where students are going, with specifics | "Prepare for SAT and AP with teachers who know the exams." | "We unlock limitless potential for tomorrow's leaders." |
| **Warm:** address students directly, respect every background | "You'll study with classmates from different international schools." | "Students are exposed to a diverse cohort." |
| **Precise:** exact programme names and real numbers | "IGCSE, AP and IB support for grades 10–12." | "Lots of international courses for all levels." |

- **Arabic examples:**

| Say | Don't say | Why |
|---|---|---|
| «حصص الـ SAT تبدأ ١١ أكتوبر. المقاعد محدودة.» | «سارعوا! فرصة لا تُعوَّض!!! 🔥🔥» | Clear beats hype |
| «نفس الفريق، نفس الأساتذة، ونفس الرقم، باسم جديد.» | «انتهى عهد وبدأ عهد جديد» | Continuity, not rupture |
| «نتائج طلابنا في AP 2026» (with real, consented numbers) | «نتائج مضمونة» | Integrity: results are never guaranteed |

## Register 2: Internal (to Fahed and the team)
- Decision first, then the reason, then the detail.
- Short bullets. No filler, no flattery, no "Great question".
- Always state what is done, what is blocked and what needs his approval.
- Use the four-part task intake shape for new work (see `/CLAUDE.md`).

## Hard rules
1. **Programme names** always in capitals, exactly: IELTS, TOEFL, SAT, ACT, EST II, IGCSE, AP, IB. Check full names with the academic team before publishing.
2. **Name:** "United International Academy" in full on first mention. "UIA" after that is fine internally and in hashtags. Never retype the wordmark as text in a design; use the logo file.
3. **Old name:** "Formerly Success 4Sure – Khalda" only as a small secondary line, only during the transition window, and only if legally cleared (decision D-01). Never write it larger than or before the UIA name.
4. **Phone:** one number, standard formats only (see `05_Operations/phone_number_standard.md`). Never the second number in marketing unless D-05 says so.
5. **Banned words and claims:**
   - "guaranteed results" / «نتائج مضمونة»
   - "100%"
   - "the best in Jordan/the Middle East"
   - "elite"
   - any statistic we can't document (e.g. "80% scored 5") until verified
   - "school" (until decision D-02)
6. **Offers:** no rolling or permanent discounts ("50% OFF", "before 1/10", "before 1/11"…). Offers are rare, dated and approved (Tier 2). Constant discounting contradicts a premium brand.
7. **Emoji:** at most 2–3 per caption, used as signposts (📍 📞 📅). No emoji walls.
8. **Hashtags:** 3–6 relevant ones. Check the audience fit (no #Tawjihi on international-curriculum posts).
9. **Visuals:**
   - real UIA students and teachers only, never stock
   - natural light
   - gold used as one emphasis per layout
   - symbol bottom-left on posts
   - alternate statement, photo and announcement templates
10. **Never** publish anything with placeholders, mismatched dates, or a typo in a programme name.
11. **Never** mention the separation, the previous network's issues, competitors by name, or blame.

## Transition phrasing (approved direction, final copy needs Fahed's sign-off)
- EN: "Same team. New identity. Bigger vision. New chapter."
- AR: «نفس الفريق. هوية جديدة. رؤية أكبر. فصل جديد.»
- The feeling to create: *"They didn't disappear. They evolved."*
