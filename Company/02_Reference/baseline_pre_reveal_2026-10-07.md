## 5. Data quality flags
1. **IG views duplicated:** 5 Oct and 6 Oct both show exactly **7,359** views. Likely a sync artifact. Treat 6 Oct views as *unverified*.
2. **Follower maths does not reconcile exactly:** gained − lost (22 Sep–5 Oct) = −76 vs. the change in follower total = −66 over a similar window. A ~10-follower gap is normal for Meta reporting. Use the **follower total** as the truth for alerts.
3. **Reach sums are not unique people.** Summing daily reach double-counts people seen on several days. Compare like-for-like only (same method before and after).
4. **The 25–27 Aug spike (+4,959)** is still inside the follower total. The slow net loss since (≈ −4/day) is consistent with low-quality followers churning. The reveal may speed this up. A higher unfollow rate in week 1 is *expected*, not necessarily a brand problem.
5. **Rename risk:** Metricool should track by account ID, so a username change should not break history (*assumption*). Verify on 8 Oct evening (brand_change_kit §3 item 8). If it disconnects, this file is the only clean "before".
