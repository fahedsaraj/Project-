# UIA Brand Guidelines — Edition 1.0, October 2026 (21 pp) — source of truth. Summary read in full 2026-10-07.
Who: international academy for international students in grades 10, 11, 12 (ages 15–18) from many countries/curricula, and their families.
Programmes (always CAPITALS, check full names with academic team before publishing): IELTS, TOEFL (English language); SAT, ACT, EST II (university admission); IGCSE, AP, IB (curriculum); "and more".
Purpose: prepare every student for a place at a university anywhere in the world.
Promise: Three focused years, one shared community, and a clear path beyond graduation.
Personality: Confident, precise, warm and international. Ambitious without being elitist.
Line: "Shaping Global Minds."
Story: U, A, I interlock = different students, one academy; mortarboard = achievement; Gold = excellence.
Values: Excellence (high standards + support), Global outlook, Community (students, families, teachers), Integrity (honest advice, clear results, respect for every background). Every communication reflects at least one.
Logo: symbol + wordmark (UNITED in Archivo widest; INTERNATIONAL ACADEMY spaced to same measure). Lockups: stacked (default), horizontal (web headers, fascias, EMAIL SIGNATURES, lanyards), symbol (avatars, favicons). Full colour on white/Paper; reversed on Midnight. Mono: blue, gold (foil/thread), black, white. Clear space x = height of UNITED letters. Min: stacked 30mm/120px, horizontal 45mm/180px, symbol 10mm/24px. Never redraw/retype, stretch, rotate, recolour, low contrast, effects, rearrange, busy images.
Colours: Academy Blue #29566C; Academy Gold #E2A02D (sparingly, one emphasis per layout, never background for long text); Midnight #0B1626 (dark ground); Platinum #D8DADF; Charcoal #26292E; Paper #F5F4F0. Proportion: Midnight > Blue > white > Platinum > Gold.
Type: Archivo (text, labels SemiBold caps +180 tracking; body Regular 10–12pt, 150%); Newsreader (headlines Regular 24pt+; quotes Italic in blue or gold-deep). Google Fonts.
Voice: two readers (students 15–18 + parents). Clear ("IELTS classes start on 15 March. Places are limited." not "Prospective learners are encouraged to register in a timely manner."); Ambitious with specifics ("Prepare for SAT, ACT and AP with teachers who know the exams." not "We unlock limitless potential for tomorrow's leaders."); Warm ("You'll study with classmates from around the world." not "Students are exposed to a diverse cohort."); Precise ("IGCSE, AP and IB for grades 10–12." not "Lots of international courses for all levels.").
Photography: own students grades 10–12, never stock; natural daylight; space for logo/headline; eye level; avoid posed group smiles, heavy filters, clutter.
Stationery: business card 85x55 450gsm uncoated, front Midnight w/ reversed symbol; letterhead A4 120gsm horizontal logo top-left 20mm margins, gold rule footer.
Digital: avatar = reversed symbol on Midnight circle; posts alternate statement / photo / announcement templates, symbol bottom-left; web horizontal logo in header, gold for one CTA per screen. Mock IG handle: unitedinternationalacademy, bio "International school · Grades 10–12".
Signage: fascia horizontal reversed on Midnight, gold base line, illuminated; crest gold thread on navy 60mm; lanyards staff blue, visitors gold.
Certificates: stacked logo + gold keyline. Merch: symbol or reversed logo on Blue/Midnight (notebook, pin badge, hoodie).
GAPS: no Arabic name/typography/voice; no transition/"formerly" rules; placeholders for brand enquiries email, address, website; Pantone/CMYK to confirm on proofs; no master logo files in workspace.
