## Rebrand transition brief (received 7 Oct 2026)
We have gone through a major business transition. The previous branches have separated, and we are now building a completely new brand: United International Academy.
I will provide a PDF containing the new brand guidelines and identity details. Treat this PDF as the primary source of truth for the new brand and use it whenever making decisions about the brand, visual identity, communication, content, or marketing.
Our Primary Goal
Our first major marketing objective is to successfully transition from the current brand to the new brand.
This is not simply a name or logo change.
We need to move our existing audience, students, parents, customers, communication channels, and brand presence toward United International Academy while maintaining trust and continuity.
The most important message is:
The brand is changing, but the team, experience, and commitment to our students remain.
We do not want our existing customers to feel that they are being abandoned, that the academy has disappeared, or that they need to start over with a completely unknown organization.
We want them to understand:
Same team. New identity. Bigger vision. New chapter.
First Priority — Email
We will begin the transition with our official business email and digital identity.
The email setup should be professional, secure, organized, and owned by United International Academy.
The transition should include:
* Creating the official business email
* Establishing the correct email structure
* Setting up professional email signatures
* Moving relevant business communication to the new identity
* Maintaining continuity with existing customers
* Clearly communicating the brand transition
* Making sure important contacts are not lost
* Ensuring the new email feels established and professional from day one
Customer Retention
One of the biggest priorities is retaining our existing customer base.
We should not treat the rebrand as an opportunity to start from zero.
Instead, we should use the existing trust, relationships, student history, and audience as a foundation for the new brand.
The transition strategy should answer the customer's questions before they have to ask:
* What happened to the previous brand?
* Is this the same team?
* Are the teachers still the same?
* Are our courses and services continuing?
* Do we need to do anything?
* Can we still contact you?
* Why did the name change?
* What is different?
* What is staying the same?
* What does the new brand mean for us?
Communication Strategy
The transition must feel intentional, positive, professional, and reassuring.
Do not communicate the separation or internal conflict in a negative way.
Do not discuss internal disagreements, blame, or business conflicts publicly.
Instead, position the change as:
An evolution. A new chapter. A stronger identity. A broader vision. A continued commitment to our students.
The audience should feel:
"They didn't disappear. They evolved."
Transition Campaign
Build a structured transition campaign from the previous identity to United International Academy.
The campaign should include, where appropriate:
1. Awareness — Introduce the idea that something is changing.
2. Reassurance — Explain that the team and commitment remain.
3. Reveal — Introduce United International Academy.
4. Story — Explain why the new identity represents a bigger vision.
5. Migration — Direct the existing audience to the new email, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, website, and other official channels.
6. Reinforcement — Continue reminding the audience about the connection between the previous identity and the new brand until the transition is fully understood.
Brand Transition Principle
Never make the audience feel that they are losing something.
Make them feel that they are moving forward with us.
The communication should create continuity:
Previous Brand → Same Team → New Identity → Bigger Vision → United International Academy
What I Expect From the Marketing Team
Do not immediately start creating random posts.
First, understand the transition strategically.
Analyze:
* What existing assets and audiences need to be transferred
* Which communication channels need to change
* What customer information must be preserved
* How to communicate the transition
* How to minimize customer confusion
* How to retain existing students and parents
* How to build recognition for United International Academy
* How to connect the old identity with the new identity without weakening the new brand
Then build and execute the transition systematically.
The ultimate objective is:
Launch United International Academy as a new, professional, modern international education brand while preserving the trust, relationships, and customer base we have already built.
