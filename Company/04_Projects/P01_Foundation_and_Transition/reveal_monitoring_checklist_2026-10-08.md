## 2. Thresholds: when to alert Fahed
**🔴 Red: tell Fahed immediately (Atlas calls or messages, any hour 08:00–23:00)**
- **Any** comment, DM or parent message mentioning the separation, the previous network, a dispute, or "who owns" → Quill does **not** reply publicly. Hide only if abusive (Fahed decides). Escalate.
- **Any** existing parent who says they are worried, confused or want to withdraw → Keeper calls within 1 hour; Fahed is told.
- Instagram or Facebook shows an **impersonation/account warning**, a **name-change rejection**, or **login/ownership problems**.
- **Followers lost > 60 in any 24 h** (≈ 6× normal, ≈ 1.3% of followers), or **net loss > 120 over 8–10 Oct** (≈ 2.7%).
- **A scheduled post or ad goes out with the old name, a placeholder or the wrong phone number.**
- **Zero** new WhatsApp enquiries on a full working day (9 or 10 Oct) *and* the reveal reel is live. This may mean the number or link in the bio is wrong. Check the link first.

**🟠 Amber: include in the next scheduled check-in**
- Followers lost **25–60 in 24 h** (≈ 3–6× normal).
- **Reveal reel reach < 500 at 24 h** (below the bottom third of recent reels; median ≈ 750).
- **Negative or confused comments ≥ 20%** of all comments on the reveal posts, or **≥ 3 "is it the same place/number?"** questions → Quill adds a pinned reply / FAQ line.
- **Parent reply rate < 30%** by T+24 h, or **> 10%** of messages not delivered (wrong numbers) → Keeper follows up by phone.
- Metricool stops updating after the rename → Forge reconnects (Tier 1).
- Any first reply to a WhatsApp enquiry taking **> 15 min** in working hours.

**🟢 Green: good news, report in the summary**
- Reveal reel reach **≥ 2,100 at 48 h** (top-3 level), or shares **≥ 60** (contest level).
- Net follower change **≥ 0** over 8–10 Oct (beats the −4/day trend).
- WhatsApp enquiries clearly above the normal day (normal is *unknown* until the tally starts, so the first week sets the baseline).

*Thresholds are set from 4 weeks of Metricool data (baseline file §6). They are first estimates; Compass reviews them after 10 Oct.*
