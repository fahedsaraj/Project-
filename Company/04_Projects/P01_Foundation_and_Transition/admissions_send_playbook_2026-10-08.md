## 3. Step 1: broadcast lists (fastest, for people who saved our number)
**The rule:** a broadcast is delivered **only to people who saved +962 79 055 5890 in their contacts.** Everyone else silently gets nothing. Replies arrive as normal individual chats.
1. Make sure the parents are **saved as contacts on the academy phone** (needed to select them).
2. Phone: Chats → ⋮ (Android) or "Broadcast Lists" (iPhone) → **New broadcast**.
3. Build lists by language: `8Oct AR 1`, `8Oct AR 2`, `8Oct EN 1`. **Up to 100 parents per list** (WhatsApp allows 256; smaller lists are easier to check). *Estimate, conservative on purpose.*
4. At ⟦T⟧ + 30 send to each list: **message 1 = reveal video** (`reveal_pack/video/uia-reveal-reel-1080x1920.mp4`), **message 2 = §C text** in that list's language.
5. **At ⟦T⟧ + 45, check who got it:** long-press the text message in the broadcast → **Info** → "Delivered to" / "Read by".
   - Name appears → in the Sheet: `notified_8_oct = Yes`, `notified_method = Broadcast`, `saved_our_number = Yes`, time, sender.
   - Name missing → `saved_our_number = No`, and send individually (§4).

## 4. Step 2: individual messages (everyone the broadcast didn't reach)
- Open the parent's chat (search their name, or start a new chat with their number), send **video**, then **§C text** starting with their name: «مساء الخير أ. ‹name›،» / "Good evening ‹name›,". *(‹name› = replace with the real name before sending; never send the brackets.)*
- **Batches of 20, then a 2–3 minute pause.** Many identical messages very fast to people who haven't saved the number can get the business number restricted, and **this number never changes**, so don't rush it. *Estimate: about 1 message per 40 seconds per sender, so two senders cover ~150 parents in an hour.*
- Log each one immediately: `notified_8_oct = Yes`, `notified_method = Individual`, time, sender.
- **Not on WhatsApp / message fails** (one grey tick for more than 30 min, or "not on WhatsApp"): `notified_8_oct = Failed`, then **call** the parent before 20:30 with the same message in your own words; log `notified_method = Call`.

## 6. When to escalate to Fahed (immediately, by WhatsApp or call)
| Situation | Why |
|---|---|
| A parent is upset, complains, or threatens to withdraw | Personal call from management protects the relationship |
| Questions about the separation, the previous network, legal or ownership | Only the approved FAQ line is allowed (#5) |
| Fees, refunds, contracts, payment or withdrawal | Pricing and legal decisions are Tier 2 |
| A parent objects to their child being filmed, or asks to delete footage | Consent of minors |
| Someone from the media, another centre, or a stranger asks for details | Public-facing; not ours to answer |
| WhatsApp shows a warning, restriction, or messages failing in bulk (10+) | Protects the business number |
| At ⟦T⟧ + 80, more than 10% of current parents are still not notified | Fahed decides whether to hold the public go-live |

**Escalation message format** (one line, no screenshots of other families):
> `ESCALATE · ‹parent name› (‹student name›) · topic: ‹fees/why/complaint…› · what they said: ‹short› · my holding reply: sent/not sent`
