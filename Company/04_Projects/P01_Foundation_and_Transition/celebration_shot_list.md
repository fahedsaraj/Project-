# Celebration shot list: Thu 8 Oct 2026 (Lens, for Fahed's approval)

⟦T⟧ = celebration time set by Fahed (17:00 proposed), as in `reveal_day_plan_2026-10-08.md`.

**Goal:** footage for the weekend recap reel (20–30 s) and a few photos for stories. About 25 minutes of filming, all on a phone.
**Consent rule (students are 15–18):** film freely, but publish only shots marked ✅. Shots marked 🔒 stay in the archive until the parent's written consent is logged.

## Before ⟦T⟧: setup (5 min)
- **Two phones.** Fahed is speaking at the celebration, so he can't film it. **Second camera: one teacher or admin, named tonight.** Phone A (operator) covers the room; phone B (Fahed, or a second teacher) shoots details before and after.
- Settings: **vertical 9:16, 4K 30 fps** (or 1080p 60 fps for slow motion). Clean the lens. Do Not Disturb on. At least 10 GB free.
- **Lock exposure on the screen** (press and hold until "AE/AF Lock" shows, then drag the brightness down slightly). Otherwise the screen blows out when the lights go down.
- Hold each shot **5–8 seconds**, steady, with no zoom. Move your feet instead of zooming.

## Don'ts
- No posed group smiles at camera as the hero shot (guideline). Real moments only.
- No filming students' names, ID cards or registration screens.
- Don't post anything live from the room. Public posts go up only after the parent message (⟦T⟧ + 30 min).
- Never upload raw footage to a third-party app. Edit on the academy phone or laptop and share only through the academy drive.
