## How to use
- **Reply in the language of the comment.** If mixed or unclear, Arabic first.
- **Public comment = short + move to WhatsApp/DM.** Details (prices, schedules, a student's case) never go in public comments.
- **Speed:** reveal evening, check comments every 30 min until 23:00; then the normal morning/afternoon rhythm.
- **Every enquiry is a lead:** log name/handle, programme and source (`IG-COMMENT`, `IG-DM`, `FB-COMMENT`, `FB-DM`) for Keeper the same day.
- **Never:** argue, mention the separation, the previous network, reasons, other branches or competitors; quote prices publicly; promise results; confirm IELTS/TOEFL/ACT (D-03); call us a "school" (D-02).
- **Escalate to Fahed immediately** (screenshot + link, don't reply further): legal/ownership claims, a complaint from a current family, anything about staff, abuse, press or anyone claiming to speak for the previous network.
- **Hide, don't delete** spam or abusive comments (deleting is Tier 2). Never hide honest criticism; answer it once (R10).
- Max 1 emoji per reply. Phone always as **+962 79 055 5890**.
